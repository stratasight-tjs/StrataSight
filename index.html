The Data Layer

What we track
across your business.

PE firms build comprehensive data views of every business they touch. We build the same view for yours — across financial performance, operations, customers, and exit readiness.

Financial

Revenue & Growth

Top-line trends, revenue mix, growth rate, customer concentration, and recurring vs. one-time revenue splits.

Financial

EBITDA & Margins

Gross margin, operating leverage, EBITDA trajectory, and margin compression points by segment or cost center.

Financial

Cash Flow

Operating cash flow, working capital efficiency, cash conversion cycle, and liquidity runway trends.

Customer

Customer Metrics

Acquisition cost, lifetime value, churn rate, concentration risk, and net revenue retention across your customer base.

Operations

Operational KPIs

Capacity utilization, labor efficiency, throughput, process cycle times, and productivity by function.

Strategic

Market Position

Competitive benchmarks, pricing power, win rates, and share-of-wallet trends relative to your addressable market.

Financial

Balance Sheet Health

Asset efficiency, debt structure, inventory turns, and working capital ratios benchmarked to industry norms.

Exit

Exit Readiness Score

Quality of earnings, financial documentation, recurring revenue percentage, management depth, and process maturity.
