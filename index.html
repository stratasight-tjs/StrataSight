What We Analyze

A complete view
of your business.

Most business owners see a handful of top-line numbers and fill in the rest with instinct. We build the complete picture — across every dimension that influences how your business performs and how much it's worth.

Financial

Revenue & Growth

Top-line trends, revenue mix, growth trajectory, customer concentration, and recurring vs. one-time splits.

Financial

Profitability & Margins

Gross margin, operating margin, profit trajectory, and where margin is being compressed by segment or cost center.

Financial

Cash Flow

Operating cash flow, working capital, cash conversion, and liquidity trends over time.

Customer

Customer Metrics

Acquisition cost, lifetime value, churn, concentration risk, and net revenue retention across your customer base.

Operations

Operational KPIs

Capacity, labor efficiency, throughput, cycle times, and productivity by team or function.

Strategic

Market Position

Competitive benchmarks, pricing strength, win rates, and how you're tracking relative to your market.

Financial

Balance Sheet Health

Asset efficiency, debt structure, inventory turns, and working capital ratios benchmarked to your industry.

Goals

Goal & Milestone Tracking

How you're tracking against the targets that matter — revenue goals, margin targets, growth milestones, and transition readiness.
