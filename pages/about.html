About StrataSight

Great businesses deserve
better information.

We built StrataSight because too many business owners make critical decisions with an incomplete picture of their own business. We fix that — with clear data, honest analysis, and ongoing coaching that keeps you moving forward.

Why We Exist

Most owners run
their business on
feel.

That's not a criticism — it's how most successful businesses get built. Pattern recognition, gut calls, years of hard-won experience. But at a certain point, growing further — or making your business worth more — starts with understanding your own numbers more clearly than you do right now.

The problem is that most business owners don't have a clear, unified view of what's actually happening across their business. Revenue, margins, cash flow, customer trends, operational efficiency — it's all out there, spread across different systems, generating signals that nobody's reading.

We built StrataSight to read those signals. To connect the dots between every part of your business, build the picture most owners have never had, and work with you on what it means and what to do about it. Not once. Ongoing — because that's how growth actually happens.

How We Operate

What we believe
about this work.

Data Before Opinions

Every recommendation starts with your actual numbers. Not a framework borrowed from a different industry, not a gut feeling dressed up as analysis. Your business, understood on its own terms.

Built to Be Used

Analysis that sits in a folder isn't worth much. Everything we produce is designed to be acted on — clear, prioritized, and structured so that the next step is obvious, not overwhelming.

Consistency Over Events

A one-time report tells you where you were. Ongoing, regular analysis tells you where you're going. We show up every week and every month — not just when you call us.

Honest, Not Just Positive

We tell you what we see — including the things that are harder to hear. A good advisory relationship requires honesty about what the data actually shows, not just the good parts.

Forward-Looking Always

Historical data is context. What we're really building toward is what your business looks like in one, two, and five years — and making sure the decisions you're making today point in that direction.

Business-Level Only

We work strictly at the business level — operations, financial performance, and growth strategy. That focus keeps our analysis sharp and our recommendations relevant to what you're actually trying to do.
