About StrataSight

We built the firm
we wished existed.

PE firms have been applying rigorous data analysis to businesses for decades. That discipline has always belonged to institutional capital. We built StrataSight to change that.

The Problem We Solve

Owner-operators
fly blind.
PE firms don't.

When a private equity firm acquires a business, the first thing they do isn't cut costs or restructure management. They build the data infrastructure to understand what they actually own. They instrument every process, track every KPI, and build dashboards that tell them — in real time — exactly where value is being created and where it's being destroyed.

That analysis takes months and costs significant money. Most founder-owned businesses never get it. They operate on instinct, aging spreadsheets, and a handful of top-level metrics that don't tell the full story. They often don't know their true EBITDA multiple, their customer concentration risk, or which cost centers are compressing their margins.

By the time they're ready to sell, they've left years of value creation on the table — and they usually find out too late. We built StrataSight to fix that. We give owner-operators the same analytical foundation PE firms build on day one. The same data rigor. The same performance cadence. Without the acquisition.

How We Operate

The principles
behind the work.

Data Before Opinions

Every finding, every recommendation, every insight comes from your actual numbers. We don't apply generic frameworks or recycle advice from unrelated industries. Your business analyzed on its own terms.

Built to Execute

Analysis that doesn't lead anywhere isn't analysis — it's decoration. Everything we produce is prioritized, sequenced, and structured to be acted on immediately. Not filed away for next quarter.

Cadence Over Events

A one-time report isn't how PE firms operate — and it's not how we operate. Value is built through consistent, ongoing review. We track your business on the same weekly and monthly cadence it deserves.

Business-Level Only

We work strictly at the corporate strategy level — operations, financial performance, market position, and value creation. We don't touch personal finance, investment products, or anything outside our scope.

Exit-Aware from Day One

Everything we do is framed against what a buyer would see, care about, and pay for. We're not just improving your business — we're improving what your business looks like to the market when it's time.

You Keep the Upside

We don't buy equity. We don't take a percentage of any transaction. Our model is advisory — you pay for the analysis, the roadmap, and the ongoing engagement. The value we create belongs entirely to you.
