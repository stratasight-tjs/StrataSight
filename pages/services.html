Services

The work that
moves the number.

Every engagement maps directly to enterprise value. We don't do generalist consulting or recycled frameworks. We do the specific analytical work that PE firms use to grow businesses before they sell them.

01

Data Infrastructure & Baseline

When PE firms acquire a business, the first thing they do isn't cut costs or change management. They build the data infrastructure to understand what they actually own — connecting every relevant data source into a unified performance view. Most owner-operators have never had that view.

We build it for you. We connect to your financial systems, operational data, and customer records and construct a performance dashboard that shows — in real time — exactly what's happening across every metric that matters to enterprise value. This is the foundation everything else runs on.

Without this foundation, any recommendation is a guess. With it, every decision you make going forward is grounded in real data. Most clients tell us this alone changes how they run the business.

What's Included

  • Financial system data integration
  • Operational and CRM data connectivity
  • Custom performance dashboard build
  • KPI framework and target-setting
  • Industry benchmark analysis
  • Baseline valuation advisory opinion
  • EBITDA multiple and margin analysis
  • Written baseline report and walkthrough
02

Ongoing Performance Management

A baseline is a starting point, not a deliverable. Value is built through consistent, rigorous review of how your business performs week over week, month over month — not through a report that gets filed and forgotten.

We run the same analysis cadence that PE operations teams run on their portfolio companies. Weekly data pulls, monthly deep-dives, quarterly trend reviews. Every cycle, we're looking at the same things: where are you outperforming targets, where are you behind, what anomalies appeared, and what does the trend line say is coming next.

When we find a deficit — a cost center that's compressing margin, a customer segment with deteriorating metrics, a process creating hidden drag — we surface it immediately with context, not at the end of the quarter when it's already priced into your results.

What's Included

  • Weekly data review and anomaly alerts
  • Monthly performance report and check-in
  • Goal tracking and variance analysis
  • Revenue and margin trend monitoring
  • Cash flow and working capital tracking
  • Customer metric surveillance (churn, LTV, CAC)
  • Operational KPI review by function
  • Quarterly trend analysis and strategic summary
03

Value Growth Advisory

Tracking performance tells you where you are. Value Growth Advisory tells you what to do about it. This is where we convert all of the data we've gathered into a prioritized roadmap of high-leverage improvements — ranked by impact on enterprise value and feasibility of execution.

We identify your specific value gaps — the places where your business underperforms its potential — and build the playbook to close them. EBITDA improvement. Multiple expansion. Customer concentration reduction. Recurring revenue growth. Exit readiness scoring. Everything framed by the same question a buyer would ask: what is this business actually worth, and why?

The output isn't a deck to present. It's a working document with specific initiatives, owners, timelines, and the metrics we'll use to track whether it's working. We stay engaged through execution — not as an advisor who delivers and disappears.

What's Included

  • Value gap identification and prioritization
  • EBITDA improvement roadmap
  • EBITDA multiple expansion strategy
  • Revenue quality and mix optimization
  • Customer concentration risk reduction
  • Exit readiness scoring and gap plan
  • Prioritized initiative roadmap with milestones
  • Ongoing execution tracking and advisory
