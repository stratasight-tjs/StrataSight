01

Business Analysis & Baseline

Most business owners have a general sense of how their business is doing. Revenue is up or down. The team feels stretched or comfortable. A few key numbers get checked weekly. But that's a partial picture — and partial pictures lead to partial decisions.

We start every engagement by building the complete view. We connect to your financial systems, operational data, and customer records and bring it all together in a unified performance dashboard that shows exactly what's happening across your business — not just the numbers you already watch, but the ones that explain them.

From there, we establish a baseline: where your business stands today, how it's trending, and where the biggest opportunities and gaps are. That baseline becomes the reference point for everything we do going forward — and most clients tell us this alone changes how they run the business.

What's Included

  • Financial data integration and organization
  • Operational and customer data connection
  • Custom performance dashboard build
  • KPI framework and goal-setting
  • Industry and peer benchmarking
  • Business health assessment
  • Gap and opportunity identification
  • Written baseline report and strategy walkthrough
02

Ongoing Review & Coaching

A baseline tells you where you started. What actually moves a business forward is consistent, structured review of how it's performing week over week and month over month — catching things early, adjusting when you're off track, and building the discipline that compounds over time.

We show up on a regular cadence — weekly for high-frequency data, monthly for the deeper review. Every cycle, we're looking at the same questions: where are you ahead of target, where are you behind, what changed this period, and what does the trend say about where you're headed? When something looks off, we flag it immediately — not at quarter-end when it's already a bigger problem.

And we don't just hand you data. We sit in it with you. We explain what we're seeing, why it matters, and help you make the call on what to do about it. That's the coaching part — turning analysis into decisions, and decisions into outcomes you can track.

What's Included

  • Weekly data review and anomaly alerts
  • Monthly performance report and check-in call
  • Goal and milestone tracking
  • Revenue and margin trend monitoring
  • Cash flow and working capital tracking
  • Customer metric review (retention, growth, mix)
  • Operational performance by function
  • Quarterly trend summary and forward outlook
03

Growth & Value Strategy

Tracking your business tells you where you are. Growth strategy is about deciding where you want to go — and building the clearest possible path to get there. This is where we take everything we know about your business and turn it into a forward-looking plan.

We identify your highest-leverage opportunities: the gaps between where you are and where your business could be, ranked by impact and feasibility. We build a roadmap — specific, sequenced, and tied to the metrics that tell you whether it's working. We provide the visuals, the analysis, and the supporting materials you need to make decisions confidently and bring your team along.

The deliverable isn't a presentation that gets filed away after the meeting. It's a working document — a plan with milestones, owners, and tracking built in. And we stay engaged as you execute it, adjusting as the data changes and helping you stay on course when things don't go exactly as planned.

What's Included

  • Gap and opportunity analysis
  • Revenue and margin growth roadmap
  • Prioritized initiative list with impact estimates
  • Customer and channel growth strategy
  • Operational improvement recommendations
  • Visual materials and supporting analysis
  • Milestone roadmap with tracking built in
  • Ongoing execution support and advisory
